# 4. Attributes, skills and checks

Every check is a visible percentile roll: chance = (Attribute × 5) + (Skill × 10) + modifiers, rolled on d100 equal or under. Small numbers, visible odds, and stress that degrades performance the way it does in real life.

```
Chance = (Attribute × 5) + (Skill × 10) + modifiers
```

- **Clean success:** a roll at or under 10% of your chance gives a bonus outcome.
- **Always fails:** 95 to 100. Experts still get unlucky.
- **Example:** Perception 6, Evidence 2 = 50%. Good flashlight +5, trampled scene −15 → 40%.

## Attributes (1–10)

A rookie typically sits between 3 and 7; 8 or more is exceptional.

| Attribute | Governs | Derived stat |
|---|---|---|
| Fitness | Strength, endurance, pursuits, fighting, surviving wounds | Wound threshold, Stamina |
| Nerve | Fear, functioning under fire, resisting panic and trauma triggers | Composure = Nerve × 3 |
| Perception | Threats, hands, weapons, lies, scene details | Reaction (with Nerve) |
| Intellect | Law, procedure, reasoning, memory, reports | Case-note capacity |
| Presence | Authority, command voice, intimidation, charm, media | First impression |
| Empathy | Reading and calming people, comforting victims, rapport | Trust gain rate |

**Creation:** all attributes start at 3; spend 15 points, cap 7; origin and background add +1 or +2 to fitting attributes (maximum 8 at creation).

## Wounds, not hit points

Fitness sets the wound threshold. Injuries are Bruised, Hurt, Wounded, Critical, Dying, each with lasting penalties. A gunshot wound means weeks of light duty, physical therapy, and stress.

## Sample build: combat veteran, Boyle Heights native

- **Attributes:** Fitness 7, Nerve 7, Perception 6, Intellect 4, Presence 6, Empathy 3.
- **Skills:** Firearms 3, Defensive Tactics 2, Athletics 2, First Aid 2, Command Presence 2, Spanish 5, Streetwise 2, De-escalation 1, Report Writing 1, Law and Policy 1.
- **In play:** Firearms 80%, Command Presence 50%, De-escalation 25%, Report Writing 30%. The deadliest rookie in the division, whose use-of-force reports IA will tear apart, and who opens doors in Boyle Heights nobody else can.

## Advancement

No XP for kills. Skill points come from:

- FTO evaluations every few weeks, based on performance.
- Case milestones.
- In-service training at the academy, which costs shift time.
- Off-duty practice: range time is ammo out of your own pocket; the gym takes hours from your family.
