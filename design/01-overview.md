# 1. Overview

Rampart (working title) is an 18+ pixel-art CRPG in the Baldur's Gate tradition: you play a rookie LAPD officer in 2010, cleaning up after superhumans in the Ledger & Accords world, through an 18-month probation that can end in a career, a scandal, or a funeral.

## The pitch

You are a custom-created Police Officer I, fresh from the academy, posted to Rampart Division. Heroes get the headlines, the Ledger points and the sponsorships. Cops get the wreckage: the bodies, the looters, the missing, and the families asking why nobody came. You have no powers and no destiny. You have a gun, a radio, a training officer with secrets, and a personal life the job is trying to eat.

## Design pillars

1. **Hyperreal policing.** Real 2010 LAPD procedure, equipment, ranks and geography. Most problems are solved by talking, paperwork and judgment, not shooting.
2. **Consequences that follow you.** Every call becomes a report; every report can become a courtroom, a review board or a nightmare. Nothing resets between shifts.
3. **Ordinary people in a superhuman world.** You cannot win a cape fight. You can evacuate, triage, document, and decide whose version of the truth goes on paper.
4. **The job comes home.** Trauma, relationships, money, family and sleep are a full second game, tied to the first in both directions.
5. **Character is history.** Origin, background and training officer shape what your body does under stress, not just your numbers.

## Tone

A mix of *Southland* and *The Rookie*. The baseline is *Southland*: mundane, ugly or sad calls, many unresolved, no music cue telling you that you did the right thing. The relief valve is *The Rookie*: a class of rookies coming up together, training officers with distinct styles, station humor, and a case-of-the-week rhythm over longer arcs. Cape incidents are shot like disaster footage.

## Rating and content

Adults only. The game depicts graphic violence, death, addiction, trauma, police misconduct, sexuality and intimate relationships between adults, and moral injury. Presentation of intimate scenes and gore is adjustable (see section 16).

## Format

- **Genre:** Single-player isometric CRPG: Baldur's Gate-style exploration, dialogue and party play in real time with pause, and XCOM-style turn-based tactics (Critical Time) when a situation turns dangerous.
- **Length target:** One probation (about 18 in-game months) per playthrough; strong replay value from origin, background, FTO and ending variety.
- **Platform:** PC first; controller support for direct movement.
