# 17. Scripted events

Every handcrafted event in the game, outlined for implementation: 155 events across seven categories. Procedural calls fill the shifts; these are the written moments that carry the story, the cast and the year.

## Outline format

Each event has an ID, a title and four parts:

- **Trigger:** when and why it fires (date, act, flag, location, relationship level, or a call roll).
- **What happens:** the core scene.
- **Branches:** the player's main choices and the checks that shape them.
- **Consequences:** what changes, written as flags (like_this), meters and reputation tracks so the event can be built as data in Godot.

## Conventions

- **Fixed** events fire on a date or act beat in every playthrough. **Conditional** events need a flag, a relationship stage or a playthrough style. **Missable** events can be skipped by time, neglect or bad luck, and the game does not warn you.
- Flags persist for the whole probation. Events that read a flag name it in their trigger.
- Events marked (CW) use the content-warning system from section 16.
- Where an event depends on your FTO, it uses Ortega, Whitfield or Arakawa variants; unless stated, the scene plays with whichever FTO you have.

## 17.1 Prologue, station and training set pieces (ST)

- **ST-01 · Graduation Day.** **Trigger:** game start, fixed. **What happens:** the academy graduation at Elysian Park. Your family sits in the stands in a form set by your origin; your four classmates are in line beside you. A licensed hero flies over uninvited and the cameras turn to him instead of you. **Branches:** who you stand with for the class photo; whether you walk over to Corey Banks, the washout watching from the fence. **Consequences:** starting approval with each classmate; corey_snubbed or corey_kind.
- **ST-02 · Reporting to Rampart.** **Trigger:** first day, fixed. **What happens:** Peg checks you in at the front desk, you find your locker next to Ducks's, and you see Hartley's retirement countdown calendar. **Branches:** respectful, nervous or cocky with Peg; ask why the division left Temple Street. **Consequences:** Peg's starting attitude; asking about Temple Street sets asked_temple_st and makes Peg remember you (needed for MS-17).
- **ST-03 · Meeting Your FTO.** **Trigger:** first day; your FTO comes from academy record plus background. **What happens:** Ortega says nothing and watches how you check the car; Whitfield is adjusting a small camera in his sun visor; Arakawa has a betting slip on the dashboard and covers it with a clipboard. **Branches:** answer the FTO's first question ("Why are you here?") honestly or with what you think they want. **Consequences:** FTO approval baseline; noticing the slip (Perception) sets saw_arakawa_slip.
- **ST-04 · First Roll Call.** **Trigger:** first shift, fixed. **What happens:** Sgt. Moreland reads crime trends, a cape alert (Solstice appearing in Hollywood) and a BOLO for a gray Civic. Ducks has written "BOOT" next to your name on the board. **Branches:** laugh, fire back, or ignore it. **Consequences:** squad reputation; the gray Civic appears during the shift, and players who listened get a free stop.
- **ST-05 · The Kit Room.** **Trigger:** first shift, fixed. **What happens:** Lenny asks what you need and watches whether you inspect what he hands you. **Branches:** check the Taser cartridges and shotgun properly, sign without looking, or flatter him. **Consequences:** gear quality tier for Act 1; Lenny approval.
- **ST-06 · The Back Seat.** **Trigger:** first car check, fixed. **What happens:** under the rear seat is a baggie of Spark left by the previous shift, Calloway's crew. **Branches:** book it as found property, tell your FTO, toss it, or pocket it; or skip the check entirely. **Consequences:** booking it sets calloway_noticed_you; pocketing sets the secret pocketed_spark; skipping the check sets skipped_car_check, which later lets a suspect's contraband turn up in your car (SC-12 variant).
- **ST-07 · The First Call.** **Trigger:** first shift, fixed. **What happens:** a 415 noise complaint in Westlake turns out to be a TV left on in the apartment of an elderly woman who has been dead for days. The smell; the neighbor who never knocked. **Branches:** let your FTO lead, take the lead yourself, or handle the neighbor's guilt. Iron Stomach or a Nerve check decides whether you vomit in the hallway. **Consequences:** first minor trauma point; the death report tutorial; first_body.
- **ST-08 · First Code 7.** **Trigger:** first shift, fixed. **What happens:** a taco truck on Alvarado. Your FTO explains their philosophy of the job; from the curb you can see the fences of the Hole three blocks south. **Branches:** ask about the Hole, ask about the FTO, or eat in silence. **Consequences:** the Hole appears on your map; Code 7 spot unlocked; FTO approval.
- **ST-09 · The First Report.** **Trigger:** end of first watch, fixed. **What happens:** the report-writing tutorial on the dead woman. Your FTO returns it covered in red ink. **Branches:** rewrite it carefully, argue, or copy your FTO's phrasing exactly. **Consequences:** Report Writing practice; copying sets a mild parrot tag that defense attorneys can exploit later.
- **ST-10 · First Foot Pursuit.** **Trigger:** first two weeks, first suspect who runs. **What happens:** a 10851 suspect bails from a stolen car into Pico-Union backyards. **Branches:** chase alone into a dark yard, set a perimeter, or call the airship. **Consequences:** injury risk; the FTO's lecture either way ("Chasing alone into a yard is how cops die"); FTO evaluation.
- **ST-11 · The Wrong Shotgun.** **Trigger:** weeks 3 to 6, conditional on Ducks's hazing being active. **What happens:** Ducks has swapped the tape labels on the lethal and beanbag shotguns as a joke. **Branches:** catch it at the kit room (Perception or ST-05 diligence), miss it, or report it. **Consequences:** if missed and the beanbag is needed, a near-fatal mistake on a call, and a critical incident; Ducks's shame feeds DC-20; reporting it costs squad approval and earns Moreland's respect.
- **ST-12 · First Use-of-Force Review.** **Trigger:** first time you go hands-on or use a Taser. **What happens:** a sergeant reviews the incident while Whitlow hovers nearby. **Branches:** give a straight account, minimize, or ask for Whitlow first. **Consequences:** Credibility; Whitlow relationship; the incident enters your file.
- **ST-13 · Mid-Training Evaluation.** **Trigger:** week 12, fixed. **What happens:** your FTO and Moreland review your performance. **Branches:** accept criticism, push back, or appeal to Benning. **Consequences:** skill points; a poor result extends training by four weeks; a strong result gives you more call choice early.
- **ST-14 · The Shooting Review.** **Trigger:** your first officer-involved shooting. **What happens:** you're separated from your partner, your gun is taken for evidence, Whitlow arrives, Force Investigation Division interviews you, and you're booked with Dr. Moss and sent home for 72 hours. **Branches:** talk before the interview, wait for your rep, or call home. **Consequences:** major trauma; Credibility from consistency with your later report; ois_count +1; mandatory PL-20 session.
- **ST-15 · The Ride-Along.** **Trigger:** Act 1, fixed. **What happens:** Lt. Benning assigns a ride-along: Rachel Kim from the accountability cape network. **Branches:** cooperate, stonewall, or show her something real. **Consequences:** Media reputation; first meeting with Rachel (romance stage Acquaintance); Benning watches how you handle press.
- **ST-16 · Cutting the Cord.** **Trigger:** end of field training, fixed. **What happens:** your last shift with your FTO. They say what they really think of you; the variant depends on approval and what you know about their secret. **Branches:** thank them, confront them, or say nothing. **Consequences:** the FTO's final rating; new partner assignment for final probation; FTO relationship locks in for Act 3.
- **ST-17 · Probation Ends.** **Trigger:** 18 months, fixed. **What happens:** the day you become Police Officer II, or the day you don't. **Branches:** determined by your ending route. **Consequences:** final career state feeding the ending (MS-31).

## 17.2 Main story (MS)

### Act 1: The Rookie

- **MS-01 · The Burn Mark.** **Trigger:** Act 1, weeks 2 to 6, fixed. **What happens:** a Spark overdose at the edge of the Hole. The victim, a teenager named Beto, has blistered palms in a spiral pattern no drug produces. **Branches:** note the burns in your report, mention them to your FTO, photograph them, or ignore them; a First Aid check decides whether he lives to reach County. **Consequences:** clue burn_mark; if Beto lives, he vanishes from the hospital two days later (feeds MS-05).
- **MS-02 · Trucks on Bonnie Brae.** **Trigger:** Act 1, overnight watch, fixed date one night before MS-08's rehearsal fight (a small Act 1 skirmish). **What happens:** three Restora Group trucks parked on a residential street with crews asleep in the cabs. The next afternoon a minor cape fight wrecks that block. **Branches:** field interview the crew, run the plates, or drive past. **Consequences:** clue restora_trucks; a field interview card puts your name in Restora's files, and Castellan knows it by MS-09.
- **MS-03 · Not Their Call.** **Trigger:** Act 1, a domestic call inside the Hole. **What happens:** Calloway's gang crew arrives uninvited and takes over the scene, and a squatter is taken away in their car instead of to booking. **Branches:** object, defer, ask your FTO, or note the car number. **Consequences:** clue calloway_hole; Calloway's opinion of you (objecting lowers it, deferring raises it).
- **MS-04 · Slow Roll.** **Trigger:** Act 1, conditional on FTO Arakawa; other FTOs get a variant where a different unit is slow. **What happens:** a debris call after a fight. Arakawa takes the long way and stops for coffee. When you arrive, federal agents have already cleared the scene. **Branches:** notice the delay (Perception), say something, or let it go. **Consequences:** clue slow_roll; speaking up lowers Arakawa's approval and raises yours with Ani if you tell her.
- **MS-05 · The Report Nobody Works.** **Trigger:** Act 1, after MS-01, or a random mother from the Hole if Beto died. **What happens:** Beto's mother comes to the front desk to report him missing; the desk officer calls him a runaway. **Branches:** take a full report, take a minimal one, refer her to Father Tomás, or call County yourself. **Consequences:** clue missing_beto; full report unlocks the missing-persons thread early in Act 2; Community reputation in MacArthur Park.
- **MS-06 · Early Odds.** **Trigger:** Act 1, optional, Kramer's pool board in the break room. **What happens:** Kramer's pool already lists odds on a fight "over Westlake" a week before it happens. **Branches:** ask Kramer, bet, photograph the board, or ignore it. **Consequences:** clue early_odds; betting adds a debt to Kramer and a small secret.
- **MS-07 · Héctor Ruiz.** **Trigger:** Act 1, fixed, 415 at a Westlake building. **What happens:** a confrontation between tenants and Restora's eviction crew. Héctor Ruiz, the organizer, is loud, funny and stubborn. Restora's papers are legal. **Branches:** enforce the writ, mediate, quietly tell Héctor his rights, or take his flyer. **Consequences:** met_hector and his opinion of you; Restora's opinion; this decides how much his death in MS-08 hurts.
- **MS-08 · The 6th Street Fight.** **Trigger:** Act 1 climax, fixed (around week 16), during your shift. **What happens:** Solstice fights a "villain" called Breakwater above Westlake. Disaster footage: falling glass, a collapsing facade, screaming. You evacuate and triage under Critical Time. You can see Breakwater pull his punches, Castellan staffers arriving with a statement before the dust settles, and agents loading two uninjured people from the Hole into an unmarked van. Héctor dies under the facade. **Branches:** who you save first (triage); what you notice (Perception and Power Assessment); whether you confront the agents; whether you reach Héctor before he dies. **Consequences:** major trauma; memory cards for MS-09; clues pulled_punch, castellan_early, van_at_6th; hector_last_words if you reached him.
- **MS-09 · The Statement.** **Trigger:** after MS-08, fixed. **What happens:** a friendly Castellan representative brings coffee and a draft statement for the Ledger review that blames Breakwater for every death. **Branches:** sign it, refuse, rewrite it in your own words, or write your report first and say nothing. **Consequences:** Credibility; Castellan's attitude; your report sets the opening tone of Act 2 (section 9).
- **MS-10 · Unavoidable.** **Trigger:** one week after MS-08, fixed, at The Precinct or at home. **What happens:** the Ledger board rules Héctor's death "unavoidable" on TV. His widow, Lucía Ruiz, is shown outside the hearing. The squad toasts Solstice. **Branches:** drink with them, leave, or say what you saw. **Consequences:** squad reputation; Act 2 begins.

- **MS-19 · Ortega's Debt.** **Trigger:** Act 2, after MS-17 or MS-18. ## 17.3 Department cast beats (DC)
