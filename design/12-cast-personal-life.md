# 12. Cast: personal life

The off-duty cast is half template, half fixed: family members adapt their names, heritage and history to your origin while keeping their story beats; neighbors depend on your housing; friends, romance options and support characters exist in every playthrough.

## Family (adapts to origin and background)

| Role | Versions | Story beat |
|---|---|---|
| Parent(s) | The worrier (scanner in the kitchen, wants you to teach instead); the proud one (brags, can't hear what the job is really like); the cop parent (retired, drinks, still has friends and secrets in the department) | A parent gets sick, needs money, or needs you when you can't be there |
| Sibling | The good one (nurse or teacher with kids, tired of your absences); the trouble one (arrests, debts, addiction; eventually in your area or your holding cell); the manifested one (Tier 1–2 power, unregistered or quietly licensed) | Your badge is the manifested sibling's best protection and their biggest danger |
| Child (optional) | Maya, 6, from a past relationship; every other weekend; her other parent Jess has remarried and wants stability | Missed pickups, school plays during court dates, "why are you sad?", a custody fight if your record or drinking gets bad. Maya is one of the strongest Composure restorers in the game; losing her is one of the worst outcomes |
| Spouse (if married at start) | Chris (name and gender adjustable): married young, has their own job, learning what it means to be married to a cop | See section 13: marriage play |

## Neighbors (by housing)

| Housing | Neighbors |
|---|---|
| Koreatown apartment | Nate Holloway, 25, your roommate: struggling actor and bartender auditioning for hero biopics, always short on rent, running a pot business your badge makes very awkward. Mrs. Yoon, the landlady: watches everything, bribes you with food, asks you to "talk to" the tenant upstairs |
| Valley studio | Hank Pruitt, 70: widowed Korean War veteran next door, lonely, sharp, the one who understands a combat vet without asking. The Delgado family upstairs: loud, warm, always cooking; their son Marco, 15, is drifting toward a local gang, and you're positioned to save him or arrest him |
| Parents' house | No privacy, constant family friction, free dinners, and the shame of being a cop who lives at home |

## Friends from before the badge

- **Danny (or Dani) Reyes:** your best friend since childhood; a mechanic who doesn't trust cops, and now you are one. The clearest mirror of who you were. Arrested by your squad in Act 2.
- **Corey Banks:** academy classmate who washed out; bitter, then jealous, then hired as private security by a hero's PR firm. An inside source or a threat.
- **The old crew:** the people you drank with at 21. Every month you see less of them, and the game tracks it quietly.

## Romance options outside the job

All adults; each relationship has a built-in conflict with the job (full system in section 13).

| Character | Who | Core conflict |
|---|---|---|
| Nadia Farah, 28 | County trauma ward nurse; funny, exhausted, gallows humor | You keep bringing her the people you hurt or failed; one night it's someone she knows |
| Elliot Shaw, 30 | Public defender; sharp, idealistic; the relationship starts when he dismantles your report on the stand | Opposite sides of every case; you can't discuss work |
| Rachel Kim, 27 | Accountability cape network field reporter; ambitious and relentless | Is she with you, or the story? She may not know |
| Jamie "Halcyon" Torres, 26 | Tier 2 licensed hero with minor healing powers; mid-ranked, streams sponsored patrols; sweet and insecure | Dating a hero in a squad that hates capes; later linked to a staged fight |
| Sam Okafor, 29 | Bartender at the cop bar; has heard every story and watched cops fall apart | Won't stand by and watch you drink yourself to death |
| Someone you met on a call | A witness or victim you helped (never a suspect) | Against policy and ethically gray; the game lets you choose it and doesn't pretend it's clean |
