# 7. Combat by background, FTO and partner

Background decides what your character can do and what their body does on its own; your FTO or partner decides what the fight around you looks like. The same alley is a different fight depending on who is standing in it.

## Background combat profiles

| Background | Unique combat actions | Combat Habit | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Combat veteran (infantry) | Controlled Pair for 1 AP; Slice the Pie (clear a corner from cover); never loses a turn to surprise; Suppressive Fire is in the menu and always out of policy | Engage the Threat | Triggers, policy violations, report language |
| Combat medic | Field Tourniquet for 1 AP; Triage Scan; no Composure loss treating wounded | Run to the Wounded | Average with firearms |
| Military police | Detainee Control (one fewer cuffing step); Stack and Enter with a partner | Dominate | Quick to go hands-on |
| Non-combat veteran | Discipline: Composure falls more slowly | Cover First | No offensive edge |
| College graduate | Read the Situation: reveals suspect disposition and state | Book Answer | Weak in gunfights; slow under surprise |
| EMT or firefighter | Scene Size-Up: hazards shown before they trigger; gore costs no Composure | Run to the Wounded | Crosses a line of fire to reach a casualty |
| Corrections | Weapon Retention bonus; Control Hold (fast takedown to restraint) | Control the Cell | Poor firearms; escalates fast |
| Bouncer or security | Clinch (grappling at contact); Read the Crowd | Hands On | Ends up in knife range |
| Cape industry | Power Assessment in combat; Debris Prediction | Find the Camera | Weak in conventional gunfights |
| Trades or blue-collar | Brute Force (breach doors, carry a partner); Mechanic's Eye (disable a vehicle) | None strong | Blunt commands escalate scared civilians |
| Cadet program | Clean Radio (backup one turn faster); Policy Instinct (warning before an out-of-policy action) | Book Answer | Least prepared for chaos |

## Training officers in a fight

| FTO | How they fight | Team action | Effect on you | What angers them |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dolores Ortega | Methodical contact-and-cover; she talks, you cover; backup early, never advances without it | Coordinated Command: +15 de-escalation | Fewer, controlled fights; you stay in policy; real police tactics | Breaking formation, unjustified draws, unnecessary rounds |
| Marcus Whitfield | Moves first, flanks, jumps hot calls; Firearms 5; Engage the Threat strength 2 | Bounding Overwatch; Breach | Most combat, fastest growth, Habits reinforced; tactics that work but don't survive review | Hesitation, freezing, a statement that hangs him out |
| Ken Arakawa | Talks; De-escalation 5; slow to draw; low Fitness | Good Cop: suspect disposition drops each turn he talks | Fewest fights; when one happens, you're alone for the first turn and alone on foot pursuits | Unnecessary force, cruelty |

Arakawa's slowness is sometimes deliberate: on calls tied to the fight-fixing, he delays on purpose (section 15).

## Partner AI

Every partner is five values plus approval: **Aggression** (how fast they escalate), **Discipline** (roles and policy), **Talk** (how long before acting), **Loyalty** (whether they back your account afterward), **Skill**. Approval decides whether they follow your requests or override you.

## Combinations

| Pairing | Result |
|---|---|
| Combat veteran + Whitfield | The most lethal pairing: many rounds, very effective, heavy policy and trauma cost; a mentor who sees himself in you |
| Combat veteran + Ortega | Constant friction; she slowly trains the soldier out of you, and if it works you become the division's best cop |

## One scene, three pairings

A man with a gun in a Pico-Union courtyard; a child's bike on the ground.

- **Combat veteran + Whitfield:** both of you see the gun on turn one and fire. The suspect is dead in 1.5 seconds; nine rounds, two through an apartment window; months of investigation.
- **Cadet + Ortega:** you freeze a turn; Ortega is already giving commands; your clean radio brings backup fast; the gun drops on turn four. Nobody hurt, and she grades your hesitation harshly anyway.
- **EMT + Arakawa:** he talks the gun down, a passing siren brings it back up, he's a turn late, and you're alone with a 55% Taser or your pistol.
