# 13. Relationships and romance

Relationships are a full layer tied to shifts, trauma, secrets and the main plot; romance is never isolated from the job. All romance options are adults, and intimate scenes are part of the 18+ build with an adjustable presentation.

## Meters

| Meter | Grows with | Falls with |
|---|---|---|
| Spark (attraction) | Flirting, dates, shared interests | Neglect |
| Trust | Honesty, reliability, showing up | Lies, no-shows, secrets discovered (sharply) |
| Strain | Missed dates, trauma conditions, danger, secrets | Time together, counseling, honesty |

High Spark with low Trust is passionate and unstable; high Trust with high Strain is solid and exhausted. Strain doesn't mean less love; it means close to breaking.

## Stages

Acquaintance → Interest → Dating → Exclusive (the squad and family find out) → Serious (moving in, meeting families) → Long-term (engagement, or deciding to stay together through whatever comes). At any point: Strained, On a break, or Over. Exes stay in the world: on calls, at the bar, in court.

## Flirting

**Charm** uses Presence (confidence, humor, boldness); **Connection** uses Empathy (listening, noticing what matters). Each option responds to different approaches: Nadia to gallows humor, Elliot to honesty and argument, Sam to directness (charm does nothing).

## Dates

Pick a place, a time that fits your shift rotation, and a budget on a rookie's salary minus rent, car payments and family needs.

| Location | Mood | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Echo Park Lake | Quiet, romantic | In your division; you may meet someone you arrested |
| Griffith Observatory | Classic LA, city lights | Cheap and memorable |
| Dodger Stadium | Fun, loud | Easy conversation; fireworks nights trigger veterans |
| Koreatown karaoke | Playful | Joe knows the best rooms |
| Silver Lake bars | Trendy | Full of activists who don't like cops |
| Santa Monica beach | Relaxed day off | Long drive, whole day |
| Taco trucks on Alvarado | Casual, cheap | Perfect for Nadia or Sam |
| Hollywood Bowl | Big romantic night | Expensive; heroes everywhere |
| Home-cooked dinner | Intimate | Shows effort; needs cooking skill or a takeout lie |

On a date: calls and call-outs (a court change, a squad emergency, a shift to cover); off-duty incidents (a robbery in the restaurant, a cape fight on the horizon: cop or date?); triggers in front of someone you're trying to impress; being recognized by someone from a call.

## Between dates

Texts arrive on flip phones and BlackBerrys mid-shift. Answering on shift risks FTO disapproval or a distracted mistake; not answering costs Trust, especially after a news report about a Rampart shooting. Voicemails you're too tired to return pile up.

## What you share

| Choice | Effect |
|---|---|
| Share | Trust and intimacy; they help carry your trauma; may break department rules and pull them toward the danger |
| Edit | Safe; distance builds slowly |
| Withhold | "It was fine." Trust erodes, and they find out eventually |

Specific risks: Elliot can't hear about cases that might reach court; Rachel might use it; Halcyon is part of the Ledger world you're investigating; Nadia sees the bodies anyway.

## Intimacy

- Scenes unlock as relationships deepen, or as casual encounters with characters open to them; always between adults and always consensual.
- Tone matches the relationship: tender, funny, desperate after a bad shift, awkward.
- Presentation setting: fade to black, suggestive, or explicit (section 16).
- Mornings after carry weight: something said in vulnerability, mismatched expectations, a shift in two hours.
- Trauma shows honestly: nightmares, flinching, pulling away; partners who stay patient and partners who can't.

## Relationships and trauma

A healthy relationship is the strongest recovery source in the game. Irritability, numbing and avoidance appear as forced dialogue. Couples counseling unlocks at Serious: expensive, slow, effective. In Act 3 the conspiracy can threaten your partner directly.

## Workplace romance

- **Classmates (Tiana, Wyatt, Ani, Joe):** allowed, messy, and the whole station knows within a week; Peg knows first. A breakup means still sharing a squad and a locker room.
- **An FTO:** possible, career-ending if discovered, and the power imbalance is treated seriously.
- **Dispatcher Nine:** a slow burn, voice only for a long time.

## Multiple relationships

Cheating is possible; discovery depends on care, witnesses and gossip, and in a station someone always talks. Some characters (Sam, Halcyon) are open to non-exclusive relationships if asked honestly; others feel betrayed by the question. One-night stands from the cop bar or Silver Lake nightlife restore a little Composure and sometimes come back into your life.

## Starting married

The system becomes a marriage simulation: the stakes are keeping someone, not winning them. Missed anniversaries, the fear when you're late, the distance trauma creates; affairs are possible and devastating.

## Endings

Each relationship gets its own epilogue (married, together, apart, lost), combined with your career ending. A Whistleblower ending can come with a ruined personal life and a Company Man ending with a happy family; the mismatch is intentional.
