# 14. World, Rampart, map and movement

The game builds one home division, Rampart, in full detail rather than five thin ones; other divisions appear through cases, mutual aid and off-duty life. Rampart keeps living whether or not the player is there.

## Why Rampart

- **Density:** about 165,000 residents in 5.54 square miles, with Manhattan-like density; one of the busiest stations in the country.
- **History:** the division at the center of the corruption scandal that put the LAPD under federal oversight; in 2010 that is still raw.
- **A new building over old ghosts:** the division left its Temple Street station for a new facility at 1401 West 6th Street in 2008.
- **No dashcams:** Central Bureau cars had no in-car video in 2010; it is your word against theirs.
- **Where heroes don't go:** poor, dense, immigrant-heavy, bad for a hero's image. Heroes fly overhead to Downtown and Hollywood; the wreckage lands in Rampart.

## Districts

Each district has its own community reputation track.

| District | Real character (2010) | World layer | Typical calls |
|---|---|---|---|
| MacArthur Park and Westlake | The division's heart: Central American families, vendors, fake-ID sellers, drug dealing; memory of the 2007 May Day clash | The city's open-air Spark market; three blocks south, the Hole, a ghost district leveled in 1998, squatted, never rebuilt, tied to Ortega's secret | Overdoses, robberies, vendor disputes, squatters |
| Pico-Union | Dense apartments, Salvadoran and Guatemalan families, birthplace of major LA street gangs (fictionalized in game) | Refugees from the proxy wars a veteran PC fought in; gangs recruit former child soldiers and unregistered manifesters | Gang shootings, domestics, fires in overcrowded buildings, witnesses afraid of immigration |
| Echo Park and Angelino Heights | Gentrifying: coffee shops beside old Latino families and gang turf; Victorians; the lake | Creatives, cape network staff, and hero influencers moving in; rents rising | Burglaries, noise, cultural clashes, party drugs |
| Historic Filipinotown and Temple-Beaudry | Tight-knit Filipino community, older homes | Relatives in an insurgency-torn Philippines with American bases; a quiet veterans' network | Elder abuse, scams, slow-earned trust |
| Lafayette Park and Rampart Village | Edge of Koreatown; Korean and Latino mix, towers, small businesses | Joe Park's family store; North Korean defector networks; South Korean hero media everywhere | Business robberies, extortion, community tension |
| Silver Lake (western edge) | More affluent, artsy, politically active | Activists, anti-super campaigners, accountability journalists, grief networks | Protests, car break-ins, complaints against officers |

**Neighboring divisions:** Central (Skid Row, the courts, the federal building and handler agency offices, hero corporate towers); Hollywood (studios, celebrity heroes, staged fights, premieres); Olympic (Koreatown core, created 2009; mutual aid); Northeast (the academy in Elysian Park, for in-service training).

## Map layers

| Layer | View | Use |
|---|---|---|
| City | Top-down driving on the real grid: Alvarado, Wilshire, 6th, Temple, Sunset, Beverly, Hoover, Union, Pico, Olympic | Travel, calls on the map, pursuits |
| District | Isometric explorable blocks, alleys, parks, storefronts | Foot patrol, investigation, NPCs, reputation |
| Interior | Cutaway rooms | Searches, evidence, interviews, Critical Time |

**The station hub:** front desk (Peg, walk-in complaints), roll call room, kit room (Lenny), locker room, report writing room, watch commander's office, detective squad room, holding tank, garage and lot (Big Earl, the cars, Miranda the cat).

**Off-duty locations:** your home, The Precinct, the county trauma hospital, Ruben's gym, Father Tomás's church, your family's home, and the Downtown courthouse.

## Movement

- **On foot:** click-to-move or direct control; walk, run, careful movement; Stamina from Fitness. Your partner follows in formation and comments. Code 6 foot patrol builds trust and exposes you.
- **Driving:** Code 2 obeys traffic law; Code 3 makes civilians swerve unpredictably. Traffic AI includes buses, delivery trucks, distracted drivers and pedestrians. Crashes damage the car, injure people and generate investigations. Where you park on a call matters. The in-car computer shows calls, records and the map, and is a distraction while driving.
- **Off duty:** your personal car (reliability depends on what you can afford) or the Metro Red Line from Westlake/MacArthur Park station.
- **Time:** no fast travel on duty; the pending queue grows while you drive. Off duty, travel compresses into time skips. Time is the main currency.

## The legal layer

Every door and gate shows its status on approach.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Public | Enter freely |
| Consent | Someone with authority must let you in |
| Exigent | An emergency justifies entry (screams, gunshots, smoke); justify it later |
| Warrant | Detectives and a judge; hours or days |
| None | You can still go in; the game records it, and evidence may be suppressed |

## The living district

- **Routines:** vendors set up in the morning; kids walk to school; bars and the Spark trade wake at night. Named residents remember whether you were fair and whether you came back.
- **Crime simulation:** crimes happen regardless of you; most become calls, some you witness on patrol (on-view activity), some are never reported.
- **District state** drives calls, NPC behavior and atmosphere, shown through the world (graffiti, shutters, crowds that go quiet or wave), never as numbers:

  | Value | Moved by | Effect |
  |---|---|---|
  | Crime | Arrests, gangs, economy | Call volume and danger |
  | Tension | Shootings, protests, police incidents | Hostility, riot risk |
  | Spark activity | Shipments, dealers, enforcement | Overdoses, boosted suspects |
  | Trust | Your and the squad's conduct | Witnesses, informants, tips |
  | Cape damage | Fights | Rubble, displacement, ghost blocks |
  | Gentrification | Restora purchases, rents | Evictions, conflict, changing storefronts |

- **Persistence:** fight damage stays for weeks, gets fenced by Restora, and is rebuilt or abandoned; businesses close and open; memorials appear where people died; graffiti shifts with territory; the Hole changes every act until the bulldozers come.

## Time, weather and the 2010 calendar

| When | Event | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Every day | Day, PM and morning (overnight) watches | Different Rampart: vendors and traffic; domestics and gangs; the Spark trade and empty streets |
| June | Lakers championship; Downtown celebrations turn chaotic | Rampart units sent on mutual aid |
| July 4 | Fireworks all night | Worst night of the year for veteran triggers |
| Late September | Record heat wave | Tempers and calls spike |
| Fall | Santa Ana winds, fire season | Smoke, evacuations |
| Oct–Nov | Halloween; Día de los Muertos altars across Pico-Union and Westlake | Crowds, memorials |
| December | Holidays | Domestics, robberies, loneliness calls |
| World dates | Erasure anniversary; Ledger rankings; the hero's biopic premiere | Protests, media frenzy |

Weather also includes June gloom and rare rain that brings crash after crash.

## Media layer

TVs in bars and laundromats run cape networks with casualty tickers; billboards sell the hero above Wilshire; Restora's "Rebuilding Rampart" signs hang on fenced lots; civilians stop to film; and your own incidents make the news, sometimes described wrongly in a bar while you listen.

## Off duty in the world

You are recognizable in your district: grateful, hostile, or remembering an arrest. Carry off duty or not, and live with it when a robbery happens in the store you're standing in. You still see crime: act, call it in, or walk away.

## Interface

Notebook (names, addresses, leads), phone (texts, calls), in-car computer (calls, records, map), case board (home and locker), and the radio's constant chatter.
