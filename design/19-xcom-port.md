# 19. The X-COM port

Rampart's encounter and campaign structure is a port of *UFO: Enemy Unknown* (X-COM: UFO Defense, 1994) and *Xenonauts*. All code and art are original; only the game structure and mechanics follow those games. This section records how each piece maps onto the design, and where the port overrides earlier sections.

## Two layers

| X-COM / Xenonauts | Rampart | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Geoscape | The division map (`scenes/cityscape.tscn`) | Rampart's six districts on one map, with time compression (5 sec to 1 day) |
| UFO sighting | Call for service | Generated by district, watch and call type. Dispatch information is often wrong (section 8) |
| Interceptor / Skyranger | Patrol car 2A43 | Code 2 or Code 3 response; on arrival the battlescape starts |
| Base | Rampart Station (`scenes/station.tscn`) | Roster, car crew, backup units, kit room loadouts, range time |
| Soldiers | Officers | The player, the FTO, classmates and squad regulars from section 11 |
| Funding council, monthly report | Monthly review | Calls cleared and lost, arrests, rounds fired, use-of-force outcomes, evaluation grade, overtime budget |
| Countries leaving the council | District community trust | Calls left waiting and harm to civilians cost trust in that district. Two failing months wash you out |
| UFOpaedia | Field manual | Equipment, cover, time units, legal basis, force ladder |
| Battlescape | Battlescape (`scenes/battlescape.tscn`) | Isometric, turn-based, time units |

## Battlescape rules

| X-COM / Xenonauts rule | Rampart version |
|---|---|
| Time units: 4 per step, 6 diagonal, turning, kneeling 4 / standing 8 | Same. TU = 40 + Fitness x 3 + Nerve x 2 |
| Snap, aimed and auto shots as a percentage of max TU | Fire, aimed shot (+20) and controlled pair (second shot -10) |
| Firing accuracy | Perception x 5 + Firearms x 10 (section 4), plus the section 6.3 modifiers |
| Kneeling bonus | +10 kneeling. Targets kneeling at more than 5 tiles are -10 to hit |
| Cover stops shots (Xenonauts) | Full cover -40, half -20, concealment -10 (section 6.3). Only full and half cover stop rounds |
| Misses travel on | Stray rounds deviate, go through glass, drywall and car doors, and can hit bystanders. Each is logged |
| Reaction fire (reactions x TU left) | Same contest, with police triggers: only a visible deadly threat, a runner in Taser range, or a misread object at near-zero Composure |
| Reserve TU | Reserve snap or aimed for reaction fire |
| Fog of war, 90-degree view cone, night sight range | Same: 20 tiles by day, 9 at night, more under streetlights or with a flashlight |
| Morale, panic, berserk | Composure. At the bottom the body picks from its Habits (Engage the Threat fires, Run to the Wounded runs, Cover First kneels, Book Answer hesitates) |
| Stun damage, capturing aliens alive | Taser, beanbag, baton and OC spray. The goal is custody: cuffs, not kills |
| Fatal wounds, medikit | Bleeding wounds, a three-turn bleed-out, first aid (stabilizes, stops bleeding) |
| Mission score | Custody, weapons recovered, no rounds fired and aid given score points. Suspects lost, rounds fired, strays and harm to anyone cost points |
| Abort mission | Withdraw and wait for SWAT: the scene is lost |
| Reinforcements | Radio for backup, or call "officer needs help" for faster arrival. Backup units are set at the station |

Things X-COM never had, from the design:

- **Police action menu (6.1).** Commands, calming, records checks, witness statements, pat-downs, cuffs and first aid. Each shows its TU cost, its odds and its legal basis. With Law and Policy 2 or higher, actions you lack the basis for are flagged.
- **Legal basis (6.2).** The scene's basis rises from consensual to reasonable suspicion to probable cause as facts come in. A pat-down or arrest without basis is recorded.
- **Use-of-force review (6.5).** Every application of force is compared with the threat at that moment, both as it really was and as the officer perceived it. Outcomes: in policy, tactical debrief, administrative disapproval, out of policy.
- **Suspects with dispositions and states (6.5).** Suspects surrender when their resolve breaks, flee if afraid of prison, draw hidden weapons, and ignore pain on Spark.

## Overrides of earlier sections

- **Section 6.3 action points.** Section 6.3 gives each officer 2 action points per 1.5-second round, acting in individual Reaction order. The port uses X-COM's time units instead, and a side-based turn: the officers move, then everyone else moves.
- **Section 7 partner control.** Section 7 has partners act on their own AI by default. The port gives the player direct control of every officer on scene, which the design lists as a difficulty option.
- **Section 4 advancement.** Skill gains come from what officers did on a call (arrests, commands, first aid), X-COM style. Shooting people never raises a skill. Firearms improves only through range time.

## Not built yet

- Story events playing out on the map: only ST-06 is playable, and dated calendar events appear as news.
- Report writing, court and the personal-life layer.
- Driving and pursuits.
- Saving in the middle of a battle.
- Sound.
