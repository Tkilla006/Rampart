# 11. Cast: the department

Three training officers, four classmates and a station full of regulars, each with approval, likes and dislikes, a secret, and an arc that branches on the player. Almost every secret becomes a choice the player has to make about that person.

## Training officers (Police Officer III)

Assignment follows academy record and background, as a field training coordinator would match probationers. You ride with the other two on shift swaps or after a falling-out.

### Officer Dolores "Dee" Ortega

- **Profile:** 44, 19 years on. Assigned to top-of-class graduates. Joined in 1991, worked the 1992 riots as a rookie and the Cascade years as a young officer. Divorced; three teenagers she barely sees.
- **Personality:** dry, precise, unimpressed; fails you on a missing time stamp, then quietly covers for you with the sergeant. Hates sloppiness more than corruption.
- **Skills:** Report Writing 5, Law and Policy 4, Interrogation 4, Spanish 4. Habit: Paper Trail (documents before she acts).
- **Likes / dislikes:** precision, owning mistakes, clean paperwork / cowboys, excuses, cape fans, repeating herself.
- **Secret:** in 1998 she was part of an off-the-books squad handling dangerous unregistered manifesters. A terrified 16-year-old who couldn't control his power died in custody. The handler agency buried it; she owes them, and mid-story they call the debt in, through her and then through you.
- **Arc:** she confesses and ends her career, the agency uses her until she breaks, or you take the debt off her.

### Officer Marcus Whitfield

- **Profile:** 38, 14 years on. Assigned to military and physical backgrounds. Marine, Central America tour, LAPD, Metro SWAT. Three years ago he arrested a Ledger celebrity for a drunken fight that killed a bystander; the hero's PR team and lawyers killed the charge and had him bounced back to patrol.
- **Personality:** funny, profane, tactically brilliant, openly contemptuous of capes; best person in a gunfight, worst at a press conference.
- **Skills:** Firearms 5, Defensive Tactics 4, Super-Incident Response 4. Habit: Engage the Threat (strength 2); hidden combat trauma under the jokes.
- **Likes / dislikes:** competence under fire, loyalty, dark humor, contempt for hero celebrities / hesitation, PR people, rookies who report cops, pity.
- **Secret:** a copy of the arrest's dashcam footage, proving the hero killed the bystander, is hidden in his garage. The hero's people, the handler agency and an accountability network all want it.
- **Arc:** he releases it, sells it, is killed over it, or gives it to you. With a combat veteran PC he sees himself: the best mentorship in the game, or a mirror that pulls you both down.

### Officer Ken Arakawa

- **Profile:** 51, 26 years on, two years from his pension. Assigned to rookies who struggled at the academy. Third-generation Japanese-American; grandparents interned; distant relatives died in the Erasure.
- **Personality:** warm, philosophical, endlessly patient; everyone on his beat knows him. The closest thing to a good father in the game.
- **Skills:** De-escalation 5, Interview 4, Streetwise 4, Japanese 5. Habit: Talk First.
- **Likes / dislikes:** patience, kindness to civilians, remembering names / unnecessary force, cruelty, arrogance. Oddly, never seems to dislike cape fights.
- **Secret:** a gambling problem in the cape betting markets. He owes a bookmaker tied to fight-fixers and pays by delaying responses so staged fights can play out. People have died in those fights.
- **Arc:** turn him in two years before his pension, help him get out, or look away.

## The rookie class (Police Officer I)

Posted to the same station with different FTOs. Statistically one washes out, one gets hurt and one goes bad by the end of probation; which one depends on you.

| Classmate | Who | Personal life | Secret | Arc |

## Station supervisors and detectives

- **Sgt. Gus Moreland:** field sergeant; old school, decent, terrified of consent-decree paperwork.
- **Lt. Carla Benning:** watch commander; political, loyal to her own career first.
- **Det. Ray Szabo:** homicide; pulls promising rookies onto cases and works Héctor Ruiz's death off the books.
- **Special Agent Hollis:** the handler agency's liaison; polite, smiling, very interested in you.
