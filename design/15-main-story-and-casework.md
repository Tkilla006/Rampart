# 15. Main story and casework

Rampart is being burned down on purpose: staged superhuman fights pad a celebrity hero's Ledger score, feed a betting ring, win cleanup contracts and clear land, and, at the deepest level, let the federal handler agency harvest manifesters. Every playthrough style and FTO path leads into it, and every character secret is a piece of it.

## The conspiracy's three layers

| Layer | Who | Wants | How the player reaches it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. The street | Calloway's gang crew, Spark dealers, the bookmaker | Money from Spark and fixed fights | Calls, arrests, Wyatt, recruitment into the crew |
| 2. The money | Solstice, Castellan Strategies, Restora Group | Ledger points, betting profit, cleanup contracts, cleared land | Reports, footage, property records, Szabo's case |
| 3. The power | The handler agency (Agent Hollis) | Manifesters | The missing from the Hole, Ortega's 1998 past |

## The players

- **Solstice:** a top-20 Los Angeles Ledger hero; handsome, beloved, sponsored by everyone; Net Lives quietly padded by staged fights against paid villains.
- **Castellan Strategies:** Solstice's PR firm; its people reach scenes before the coroner.
- **Restora Group:** cleanup and redevelopment; wins the contracts after every fight and buys the damaged blocks cheap; long-term target: the Hole and the blocks around MacArthur Park.
- **Victor Sarkisian:** runs underground betting on outcomes and casualty counts; Arakawa owes him.
- **Calloway's crew:** muscle, protection for the Spark trade in the Hole, paid by everyone.
- **Special Agent Hollis:** cleans up after all of them, for reasons deeper than protecting a celebrity.

## The deepest secret

Superhuman violence in a dense neighborhood can trigger latent manifestations, and the Hole is full of squatters, refugees and kids who manifested and never registered. After every staged fight, agency teams move in quietly. Strong manifesters disappear; the strongest are "elevated" into asset programs; the rest end up in a federal facility nobody can find. Ortega's 1998 squad was doing the same work when the Hole was created; the boy who died in her custody was one of the first. It has been going on for twelve years. The rest of the conspiracy does not know.

## Act 1: The Rookie (early field training)

**Purpose:** learn the division, the cast and the job; the conspiracy is background noise in ordinary calls.

**Seeded clues:** an overdose victim from the Hole with a burn that matches no drug (a manifestation sign); Restora trucks parked on a street the night before a fight there; Calloway's crew at a call that wasn't theirs; Arakawa slow on a debris call; a missing persons report from the Hole that nobody works.

**Climax, the 6th Street fight:** Solstice fights a "villain" above Westlake during your shift. Several residents die, among them Héctor Ruiz, a tenant organizer fighting Restora's evictions. The Ledger board rules his death unavoidable. You were there: the villain held back, Castellan had a statement in minutes, and federal agents took two uninjured people from the Hole. Your report (section 9) shapes how Act 2 opens.

## Act 2: The Case (late field training into final probation)

**Purpose:** investigate, get pulled between factions, watch the cast's secrets surface.

| Playthrough style | Entry point |
|---|---|
| Hot-call chaser | Calloway recruits you after shootings; from inside you see shakedowns and payoffs |
| Coaster | The Hole is beside your favorite Code 7 spot; a squatter kid talks to the one cop not in a hurry |
| Community cop | Héctor's widow and the tenant group come to you because Garza trusts you |
| Numbers cop | A routine stop turns up a runner with casualty-count odds on fights that haven't happened yet |
| Investigator | Szabo thinks Héctor was murdered and works it quietly with you |

**Investigation threads** (any order; some can be missed):

1. **The money:** Restora's purchases, Castellan's payments, Sarkisian's books; help from Szabo, Joe's network contacts, or Rachel.
2. **The fights:** proving they were staged; Whitfield's footage establishes the pattern; Joe's old network holds raw footage.
3. **The street:** Calloway's crew and the Spark trade; Wyatt is a way in and a liability.
4. **The missing:** disappearances from the Hole after every fight; the kid, Father Tomás, the families; this leads to layer 3.
5. **The past:** 1998. Peg knows; Ortega was there; Ani's father knows how off-the-books squads operated.

**Act 2 crises** (which fire depends on choices): Wyatt overdoses, is turned, or is caught (and if you covered for him, you're implicated); Arakawa is exposed by you, Kramer's pool or Sarkisian; Whitfield decides what to do with the footage and someone comes to his house; Hollis calls in Ortega's debt (make evidence disappear); Ani asks you to back her report against the gang crew or an FTO; someone in your personal life is threatened (your sibling, especially a manifested one; your child; your partner; Danny).

**Climax:** another staged fight is planned for Pico-Union and you learn of it in advance. Stop it, document it, or stay out of the way.

## Act 3: The Choice (end of probation)

The conspiracy closes in on four fronts: **the hero** (exposing Solstice means taking on the Ledger, sponsors and a PR machine; arresting a superhuman needs suppression gear and federal cooperation); **the squad** (Calloway's crew knows you know; cops protect cops until they decide you aren't one); **the agency** (Hollis offers recruitment, silence and promotion, or your personal life torn apart); **the Hole** (Restora's bulldozers are scheduled; the people there, the kid included, have nowhere to go).

## Endings

| Ending | Route |
|---|---|
| The Whistleblower | Expose everything; the conspiracy cracks; your career probably ends; symbol or target |
| The Company Man | Bury it, take the promotion; Rampart keeps burning |
| Dirty | Join Calloway, Sarkisian or both; you become what you arrested |
| The Recruit | Take Hollis's offer; learn the true scope and become part of it, or a mole inside it |
| Partial Justice | Bring down the street layer and Solstice; the agency walks; the most realistic ending |
| Burned Out | Quit, medical retirement, or fired; the story ends without you |
| End of Watch | Killed in the line of duty; the investigation dies with you or passes to whoever you trusted |

A separate personal epilogue (relationships, family safety, trauma, drinking) combines with the career ending.

## The case board

Evidence, statements, photos and documents go on a case board at home (and a smaller one in your locker). You make the connections; wrong ones lead to false accusations. A case board at home is a red flag to your partner, your family, and, if they find it, the people you're investigating.
