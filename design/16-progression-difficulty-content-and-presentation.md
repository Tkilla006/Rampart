# 16. Progression, difficulty, content and presentation

One playthrough is one probation, about 18 in-game months, paced by real LAPD phases and the three story acts; difficulty and content settings let players choose how unforgiving and how explicit that year and a half is.

## Probation timeline

| Phase | Approximate length | Story act | Player control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Academy (prologue) | Compressed into the opening | Character creation in play | None; establishes record and classmates |
| Field training | About 24 weeks, extendable | Act 1, then early Act 2 | Low: FTO and dispatch choose calls |
| Final probation | About 28 weeks | Late Act 2 and Act 3 | High: new partners, more say |
| Probation ends | 18 months from the academy | Endings | Police Officer II, or not |

**Failure states before the end:** washout (field training failed after extensions), termination (misconduct), resignation, medical retirement, death (Ironman).

## Progression channels

| Channel | Source |
|---|---|
| Skills | FTO evaluations, case milestones, in-service training, off-duty practice |
| Habits | Strengthen with use; weaken with overrides and FTO retraining |
| Reputation tracks | FTO, Squad, Command, Community (per district), Media, Federal handlers |
| Credibility | Hidden; moved by report and testimony consistency |
| Trauma | Rises with critical incidents; eased by coping |
| Relationships | Spark, Trust, Strain per person |
| Money | Salary, overtime, court pay; rent, car, family, dates, ammo |
| Doors opened | Gang crew, detectives (Szabo), motors (Reyes), Metro/SWAT path, the agency |

## Difficulty

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| Mode | Standard (lethal hits become critical wounds); Ironman (one save, permanent death, End of Watch epilogue) |
| Odds display | Show percentages (default); hide them for a rawer feel |
| Memory reliability | Standard; Hard (more distorted cards, no uncertainty markers) |
| Call pressure | Relaxed, Standard, Rampart (pending queue grows faster) |
| Habit strength | Standard; Assist (overrides cheaper) |

## Content settings

| Content | Settings |
|---|---|
| Intimate scenes | Fade to black, suggestive, explicit |
| Gore | Reduced, standard, full |
| Trauma episodes | Full (palette shifts, misperception); softened (shorter, labeled) |
| Self-harm and suicide themes | Shown or summarized; always with an opt-in warning at first appearance |
| Content warnings | Optional per-call warnings for child harm, sexual violence references, and suicide |

The game depicts police misconduct, racism and violence honestly in context and never rewards cruelty for its own sake; consequences always follow.

## Art direction

- Isometric pixel art in the Baldur's Gate tradition with zoom from block to room.
- Documentary feel: subtle sway on foot, harsh streetlight at night, flat afternoon glare, palette shifts by watch and stress.
- Stress rendering: tunnel-vision vignette, muffled audio, slowed animation, deployment-palette flashbacks.
- Interiors with cut-away roofs; courtroom in side view; reports on a pixel clipboard in pen.
- Cape incidents as disaster footage: dust, smoke, a streak overhead, the camera never cutting away.

## Audio

- The radio as the soundtrack's spine: dispatch chatter, Nine's voice, codes, the dread of "officer needs help".
- Diegetic music: car radio, taco-truck speakers, bar jukeboxes, era-appropriate LA stations (original or licensed).
- Score: sparse and ambient; silence after the worst moments.
- Auditory exclusion in Critical Time: the world goes muffled.

## Interface and accessibility

- Notebook, phone, in-car computer, case board, radio log, reputation journal.
- Visible odds on every check; a mark on Habit-driven options.
- Colorblind-safe palettes for district and stress indicators; subtitles for radio chatter; remappable controls; adjustable text size.

## Scope notes

- One fully built division with six districts, a station hub, and selected locations in four neighboring divisions.
- Procedural calls supply volume; handcrafted episode beats, signature calls and casework supply story.
- Cast: 3 FTOs, 4 classmates, about 15 station regulars, about 15 personal-life characters, 11 romance options, and the conspiracy's principals.
